A398184: numbers of maximal subsets of the n X n grid with no three collinear points, by size.
A maximal subset is one to which no further grid point can be added without creating a
collinear triple. Subsets are counted as sets of grid points; rotations and reflections
are not identified. Computed by two independent exhaustive programs (both attached to
A398172 as a398172.txt); the last entry of each row is A000755(n), the first size is
A277433(n) for n >= 3.
Aleksei Kudriashov, Sep 10 2026.
