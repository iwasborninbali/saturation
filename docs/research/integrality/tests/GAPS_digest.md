# GAPS digest — what the specification (phenomenon.py / holes.py) does NOT contain

Machine-pinned by a 36-gap unittest suite (198 tests, all green; each gap is a strict expectedFailure that
turns the build RED if the spec is fixed without updating it). Source: docs/research/integrality/tests/.

## The three most consequential

1. **The diagnostic cannot detect its own subject.** `Signature` has no field for the integer optimum, so the
   integrality gap — the definition of the phenomenon in §0 — is not representable. S2 measures LP(all)/LP(strong),
   which is equally high when LP is blind and when there is nothing to be blind to. Executable consequences:
   disjoint 5-point blocks with LP = IP score 4/4 ЯВЛЕНИЕ; the empty system scores 4/4; arcs in PG(2,q) — the
   module's own example of a SOLVED regular system — score 3/4 ЯВЛЕНИЕ; cap sets, the module's own paradigm of LP
   blindness, score 2/4 не характерно. False positive and false negative on the bestiary the module itself wrote.
   `Strength` has no third value and `verdict()` no third outcome, so 'the phenomenon is absent here' is unsayable.

2. **Two of the eight holes, and one of the five principles, ARE the target.** H4 (|S₂| ≤ (3(p−1) − |S₁|) + O(1))
   sums to 3(p−1)+C at every split; H7 (T(S) ≥ c(|S| − 3(p−1) − C)) gives 3(p−1)+C at T = 0 for every c > 0. No loss
   in the constant ⇒ H4 ⇔ H7 ⇔ the open problem. The project's own notes say it verbatim (B.12: 'not an intermediate
   hypothesis but the target itself in supersaturation form'; B.13: 'any proof of it is a proof of T1'). P4 asserts as
   an established principle exactly what H3/H4 record as missing. CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS RESEARCH: brief 8 ships H4 and
   H7 as fillable holes with 'testable now' experiments, and Q2 and Q5 are generated from them — so any 'transfer'
   answering Q2 or Q5 is a complete solution of the open problem. The brief's difficulty calibration is wrong, and any
   ranked list that places a Q2/Q5 transfer highly is ranking on a miscalibration.

3. **The one signature touching the integer truth is measured on a different system and carries no scale.**
   gain_over_construction = (5,5,6,5) is the per-p maximum over ALL k (notes B.6c: p=11 attains it at k=3, p=13 at
   k=2), while OUR_FRAME fixes xy ≡ ±1, i.e. k = −1, whose maxima 32/40/54/59 give gains (2,4,6,5) — spread 4 against
   S4's threshold 3. On the frame's own numbers the headline 4/4 becomes 3/4. Worse, S4 is an absolute spread over
   four points with no reference to system size, so a gain that is Θ(|V|) passes it. The O(1) that IS the project's
   target (α ≤ 3(p−1) + O(1)) is unfalsifiable in the diagnostic meant to certify it — and the conjecture 'gain ≤ +6
   for all p' rests on four data points at p ≤ 19 whose spread the spec's own criterion rejects.

## Structurally missing, directly relevant to Q1–Q7

- **No hole corresponds to P1** (the packing↔transversal principle that DEFINES why weak constraints are jointly
  strong: τ large ⇔ the triple hypergraph is spread out). Hypergraph containers (Balogh–Morris–Samotij,
  Saxton–Thomason), expansion-based bounds on independent sets, and entropy methods appear ZERO times in holes.py
  and ZERO times in the brief — so this route cannot come back through Q1–Q7 unless it is added deliberately.
- **No hole for B.12(iv)**: nothing in the literature bounds from ABOVE the number of directions/lines spanned by a
  dense subset of the lifted pair, and the notes state this is what a global geometric argument would need.
- **All eight holes are upper-side.** Nothing guards the premise that the gain over 3(p−1) is O(1); §6 lists (T4) as
  open. If the truth is 3(p−1) + ω(1), all eight holes are holes in a wall around the wrong building.
- **No hole names the multiplicity of extremals** (9^s maxima on one hyperbola) as an obstruction, though it is
  exactly what separates this problem from Segre's arcs (the conic is the UNIQUE completion) and from Vosper.
